# EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
 
## Working Hours
 
Employees are expected to work from **9:00 AM to 6:00 PM**, Monday through Friday.
 
## Work From Home
 
Employees may work from home up to **2 days per week** with prior manager approval.
 
## Attendance
 
Employees must maintain regular attendance and inform their manager in advance of any planned absence.
 
## Leave Policy
 
Employees are entitled to:
 
* 12 Casual Leave days per year
* 10 Sick Leave days per year
* Company-declared public holidays
 
## Notice Period
 
The notice period for resignation is **60 days**.
 
## Dress Code
 
Employees are expected to maintain a **business casual** appearance during office hours.
 
## Probation Period
 
All new employees will be on probation for **6 months** from their joining date.
 
## Code of Conduct
 
Employees are expected to:
 
* Maintain professionalism at work
* Treat colleagues respectfully
* Protect company information and assets
 
## Performance Reviews
 
Performance evaluations will be conducted periodically based on job performance, attendance, and professional conduct.
 
## Confidentiality
 
Employees must not disclose confidential company, customer, or project information to unauthorized individuals.
 
## Use of Company Resources
 
Company-provided equipment, software, and internet access should be used primarily for work-related purposes.
 
## Disciplinary Action
 
Violation of company policies may result in disciplinary action, including warnings, suspension, or termination of employment.
 
## Policy Updates
 
The company reserves the right to modify policies and procedures as required. Employees will be informed of any significant changes.
