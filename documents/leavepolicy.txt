# LEAVE POLICY
 
Employees are entitled to the following leave benefits annually:
 
* 12 Casual Leaves (CL)
* 10 Sick Leaves (SL)
* 15 Earned Leaves (EL)
 
## Leave Approval
 
* All leave requests must be submitted in advance through the company's leave management system.
* Leave is subject to approval by the reporting manager.
* In case of emergencies, employees must notify their manager as soon as possible.
 
## Casual Leave
 
* Casual Leave is intended for personal work or short absences.
* Unused Casual Leaves cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.
 
## Sick Leave
 
* Sick Leave may be availed in case of illness or medical emergencies.
* The company may request medical documentation for extended periods of sick leave.
 
## Earned Leave
 
* Earned Leave may be accumulated and carried forward as per company policy.
* Employees are encouraged to plan and utilize their Earned Leave responsibly.
 
## Notice Period
 
* Leave adjustment against the notice period is not permitted.
* Employees serving notice period are expected to complete their assigned responsibilities and handover activities.
 
## Unauthorized Absence
 
* Absence without prior approval or valid justification may result in disciplinary action and salary deduction as per company policy.
